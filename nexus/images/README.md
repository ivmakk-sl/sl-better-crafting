# Images

Nexus page images, tracked in the repo. The background is 1300x372, the gallery images are 1920x1080. The mods-grid thumbnail is not a separate file: Nexus picks it from the uploaded gallery images. The images are prepared by hand from 4K in-game screenshots taken after one click on the Small Rat Cage Package recipe. The Inventory tab holds only a watch, and the Workbench Drawer is empty.

- `sl-better-crafting-banner.jpg` - the mod page background image (1300x372): the top of the backpack panel (only the watch) and of the workbench window after the click (the 4 materials on the grid, the recipe with its counts), faded at the bottom.
- `sl-better-crafting-thumb.jpg` - title card (1920x1080): "Better Crafting" with the game's handcraft icon (`Res/icon/shougongzhizuo.png` of the web UI), beside a crop of Crafting Notes with the Tool Cabinet Package recipe and its counts (Scrap Metal 56/2, Wood Chips 71/1, Wire 35/3). Pick this as the mods-grid thumbnail.
- `sl-better-crafting-sample1.jpg` - "Craft from any storage" over the backpack, the workbench, and Crafting Notes after the click: the backpack is empty, the grid holds 1 Scrap Metal, 1 Wire, and 2 Wood Chips, and an arrow from "Scans every storage in your home" points at the counts of the recipe (Wood Chips 71/2, Wire 35/1, Scrap Metal 56/1).
