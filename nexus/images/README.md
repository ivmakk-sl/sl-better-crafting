Nexus page images, tracked in the repo. The background is 1300x372, the gallery images are 1920x1080. The mods-grid thumbnail is not a separate file: Nexus picks it from the uploaded gallery images. The images are prepared by hand from in-game screenshots.

- `sl-better-crafting-thumb.jpg` - title card (1920x1080): the name "Better Crafting" beside the workbench window. Pick this as the mods-grid thumbnail.
- `sl-better-crafting-sample1.jpg` - a full in-game screenshot (1920x1080): the recipe list with "have / need" counts that include the storage of the home, and the brown border of a recipe with enough materials.
- `sl-better-crafting-sample2.jpg` - a full in-game screenshot (1920x1080): the workbench grid after one click on a recipe, with materials from a storage on another floor of the home.
