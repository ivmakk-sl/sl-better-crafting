v1.1.1 - 2026-10-05
Added: The setting CountCache (section Performance, on by default), to go back to the counts of 1.1.0 if a count of the recipe list looks wrong.
Fixed: A slow craft with much storage in your home. On a test save with about 2600 stacks in 23 storages, the work of the game in one craft fell from about 0.66 s to 0.07 s, near the 0.05 s without the mod.

v1.1.0 - 2026-09-29
Added: A batch craft uses your home storage: its "Max" counts it, and each next craft of the batch fills from it.
Added: A dye choice fills the workbench from your home storage.
Changed: Your home storage is now a place of the game's own fill, after the game's tabs (the open tab, then the backpack, the Workbench Drawer, and the Tool Cabinets). The game picks the items in each place by its own rule.
Changed: Clear sends an item from your home storage to the open tab, not back to its storage.
Removed: The mod's own pick order (clean items first, the items that expire first before fresher items).
Removed: Support for game versions before 1.1.18153 (the Autumn Update). This version needs Survival Log 1.1.18153 or later.
